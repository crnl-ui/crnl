Cards & Tiles

Closed cards, open cards, grids, logo blocks and the tile — with its one interactivity rule.

Two card shapes and one tile. A closed card is a filled box with divided sections. An open card is a transparent container of separated filled sections. A tile is the card for anything displayed alongside others — grids, carousels, feeds. Cards never take a border and never a hand-written hover (RULES §2).

Card media

.card-media · sized by ratio, never by height

Three ratios, and no fourth. A photo band set to a fixed height reads as a photo on a phone card and a letterbox slot on a web card — the ratio holds the proportion at every width. It carries no radius of its own: the card clips its children, and a rounded image inside a rounded card notches the corners where the two curves meet (RULES §2).
