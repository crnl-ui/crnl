Interactive Surfaces

All 13 surfaces and 3 scale tiers — the hover and press mechanism, live. Hover and press everything on this page.

Anything tappable that is not a .btn, .list-row or .input-field takes a .surface-* and a .scale-* on the same element. The surface carries the themed fill and border across default, hover and pressed; the scale carries the transform. Hover and press each one below — nothing here has a hand-written :hover (RULES §2, §3).

The scale number is the size of the object, not the size of the movement — small things move more so the motion reads the same at every size. .scale-700 for cards and rows (±1%), .scale-500 for buttons and selectors (±2.5%), .scale-300 for chips and circle buttons (±3.5%).

Sectionone band of a card already divided by hairlines

This surface describes where a thing sits, not how it looks. It is transparent at rest because it inherits the card's --bg-surface — so it must span a real card's full width, with its neighbours divided by a --neutral-200 hairline. Hover the bottom band below: the wash reaches both edges, which reads as "this row of the card is tappable" rather than "there is a button in here". Drawn as a floating box inside a padded container it would look like nothing at rest — that is a mocking-up mistake, not a missing state.
